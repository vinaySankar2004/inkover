# Market

What else does this job on iPad, what it costs, and where Inkover stands. Checked on 2026-09-05 against the US App Store. Refresh before every pricing change.

## What the numbers say

Rating counts are tiny across the category. The most direct competitor has three ratings after two versions. That means two things. Demand is real but small, so this is a side product, not a business. And the category has no entrenched leader, so a clearly better product can own it with a handful of good reviews.

Every paid competitor is a one-time purchase between $2.99 and $4.99. No one charges a subscription, and none of these tools has a running cost that would justify one. A subscription here would read as greed.

## Where Inkover is different

- **Ink is attached to content.** Every other tool paints on screen coordinates. Inkover anchors each stroke to the element it was drawn on, so it survives tab switches, expanded sections, reflow and rotation. This is the feature the category is missing and the one to lead with.
- **Reading, not whiteboarding.** PageScribble sells itself to teachers presenting over Zoom. Inkover is for the person reading alone: the trail for tracing a line, the spotlight for holding attention, scribble to erase, shape snap for circling.
- **The Pencil is the only pen.** Finger drawing is deliberately absent, which gives palm rejection and a page that stays usable while you draw.
- **No export, and honest about it.** Screenshot and PDF tools already exist, including Apple's. Inkover does not compete with them and says so in the listing.

## Risks

- Apple could fold Pencil drawing into Safari the way it folded Markup in. Nothing to do about it; ship first.
- PageScribble could add anchoring. Unlikely to be quick, since it changes the storage model, but it removes the lead feature.
- The category is small. The plan is a good one-time price and word of mouth, not marketing spend.

## Pricing conclusion

Free at launch to gather ratings while the category has none, then a one-time purchase at $5.99, paid up front. One dollar above the category leader, justified by anchoring and the reading tools, still an impulse price. Recorded in [[D0011-paid-up-front]].
