# Inkover privacy policy

Effective 5 September 2026.

Inkover is a Safari extension for iPad that lets you draw on web pages with Apple Pencil. This policy describes what it does with your data, which is nothing.

## What Inkover stores

The strokes you draw are stored on your iPad, in the storage Safari provides to the extension, keyed by the address of the page they were drawn on. Your tool, colour and toolbar position are stored the same way. Clearing a page deletes its ink. Deleting the app deletes everything.

## What Inkover sends

Nothing. Inkover makes no network requests. It has no account, no sync, no server, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. It contains no third-party code.

## What Inkover can see

To draw on a page, the extension runs on every website you allow it on. It reads the position and text of page elements so that ink can stay attached to them. That information stays on the device and is used for nothing else.

## Children

Inkover collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. The history is in the public source repository.
