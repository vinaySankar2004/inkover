# D0009: Non-goals

## Context
Each of these was raised and explicitly declined by the owner. Recording them stops them from being re-proposed as obvious improvements.

## Decision
Out of scope for Inkover, with no version planned: export or print of annotated pages, screenshots, Mac and iPhone builds, sync or cloud storage, typed or sticky notes, finger drawing, pressure on anything but Apple Pencil.

## Consequences
- The list in `docs/product.md` Non-goals is the user-facing copy of this decision and links here.
- Any of these needs a new decision that supersedes this one before a spec is written.
